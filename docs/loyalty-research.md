# Restaurant Loyalty and Rewards Research: What to Build for Mink's Pizza

Research date: 2026-10-03. Scope: pizza chains (Domino's, Pizza Hut, Papa Johns, Little Caesars, Marco's, Jet's, MOD), restaurant leaders (Starbucks, Chipotle, Sweetgreen, Panera, Taco Bell, McDonald's, Dutch Bros, Chick-fil-A), platforms used by independents (Toast, Square, Owner.com, Slice, Thanx, PAR Punchh, Paytronix, Spendgo), and cross-cutting design questions. Pizza and single-location evidence is prioritized.

**Method and limits.** Several primary pages returned 403 or 503 to the fetch tool: the Starbucks 2026 press release (about.starbucks.com), Axios, QSR Magazine, Restaurant Business, TheStreet, mcdonalds.com (rewards page and terms), and pizzahut.com (terms and free-pizza page). For those programs, mechanics come from secondary pages that did load (The Krazy Coupon Lady, The Points Party, NRN, Restaurant Dive, Points Insider, The Points Guy) or from search-result summaries, and are marked *(paraphrase)*. MyMcDonald's numbers come only from search summaries of third-party guides and should be treated as unverified. Reddit was not fetched, so customer-complaint evidence comes from press coverage that quotes social posts. Paytronix's 2025 report is behind a form, so only its public landing and blog stats are used. Thanx, Spendgo and Owner.com publish little mechanical detail. Text in quotation marks was on the cited page. Effective reward rates marked "(my calc)" are my arithmetic using assumed menu prices, not published figures.

## 1. Pizza chains

### Domino's Rewards (relaunched September 2023)
- **Earn:** "Loyalty members will now earn 10 points on every order of $5 or more." The old Piece of the Pie program gave 10 points per order over $10 and only "a free pizza for every 60 points earned": https://media.dominos.com/stories/dominos-rewards-story/ , https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/dominos-adds-rewards-categories-makes-points-earning-faster/693375/
- **Ladder:** "20 points: A free dipping cup, a 16-piece order of Parmesan Bread Bites or a 20 oz. drink"; "40 points: An order of Bread Twists or Stuffed Cheesy Bread"; "60 points: A medium, two-topping pizza; pasta, Oven-Baked Sandwich; or a 3-piece order of Chocolate Lava Crunch Cakes" (same URL). PMQ: free food "after just two orders": https://www.pmq.com/dominos-rewards/
- **Positioning:** "At a time when most brands are scaling back their loyalty programs and making it more difficult to earn and redeem points, Domino's is doing the opposite." VP Mark Messing: "We want to make it easier to reward our customers and give them more options so they can get rewarded faster." (pmq.com, Sept 2023)
- **Expiration:** "points expire after 180 days of inactivity": https://lji.io/directory/dining/dominos-rewards . The 2019 Piece of the Pie terms also capped earning at one order per calendar day and excluded tips (paraphrase): https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/pizza-loyalty-programs
- **Channel:** "Uber Eats orders won't be eligible for customers to earn or redeem loyalty points" (Restaurant Dive).
- **Results:** "Domino's Rewards finished 2025 with 37,300,000 active users. Which is up almost 20% since our relaunch.": https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/23/dominos-dpz-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/
- **Effective rate (my calc):** six orders to a medium two-topping (about $14). At a $25 average order that is about 9%. At the $5 floor it is far higher, so the per-order model rewards small orders and needs a daily cap.

### Pizza Hut Hut Rewards
- **Earn:** 2 points per $1 including delivery fees, with "No points on tips or third-party delivery": https://www.pointsinsider.com/reviews/pizza-hut-hut-rewards-review . The 2019 version also counted delivery fees and excluded taxes (paraphrase, The Points Guy URL above).
- **Ladder:** breadsticks 75, cheese sticks 125, Cinnabon 150, 8-piece boneless wings 175, medium 2-topping 200, any large pizza 300 (Points Insider).
- **Expiration:** "Points don't expire as long as you have some activity on your account every 6 months." (Points Insider). Pizza Hut's own X account said the same in 2017 (paraphrase): https://x.com/pizzahut/status/899445941085773824
- **Effective rate:** Points Insider puts it at "between 9%–20%+". The 2019 program gave free breadsticks on birthdays (paraphrase, The Points Guy).

### Papa Johns Papa Rewards (Papa Dough)
- **Earn and redeem (US):** 1 point per $1, 75 points = $10 Papa Dough, excluding tax and delivery (paraphrase of search summaries of Restaurant Business and QSR, which both returned 403). The 2023 revamp moved from 1 point per $5 toward a faster earn, and points were multiplied by five in the transition (paraphrase). Restaurant Business reported about half of members redeeming, versus 21% a year earlier (paraphrase): https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/financing/papa-johns-sees-some-progress-its-revamped-loyalty-program
- **Expiration (2019 terms):** points expire after 12 months of no activity, and Papa Dough expires 6 months after issue (paraphrase, The Points Guy URL above).
- **Effective rate:** a flat 13.3% back in Papa Dough. The UK program announced its own changes: https://www.papajohns.co.uk/rewards-announcement (not loaded).

### Little Caesars
- No points program. The app runs opt-in Challenges: guests "affirmatively accept the Challenge", earn by "purchasing certain menu items and/or reaching certain spend levels", and get "Promo Codes" about "twenty-four (24) hours after successfully completing a Challenge". App orders only. "Limit one (1) Little Caesars App account per person.": https://information.littlecaesars.com/en-us/little-caesars-challenges-terms-and-conditions

### Marco's Pizza My Rewards
- **Earn:** "For every $1.00 you spend you earn 1 rewards point, based on your pre-taxed total (excluding tip and delivery charges)."
- **April 27, 2026 change:** "the spending requirement for earning rewards certificates has been reduced from $100 to $50", so members now get a $5 certificate per 50 points.
- **Expiration:** "Rewards points never expire." but "Rewards certificates expire 60 days from the date they were issued."
- **Exclusions:** no points on alcohol, gift cards, fundraisers, or "any orders placed through third-party delivery platforms": https://www.marcos.com/customer-contact-center/
- **Effective rate:** a flat 10%. The change pairs a smaller reward with a reward that arrives twice as often.

### Jet's Pizza Jet's Rewards
- **Earn:** "earn 10 Points for every $10 spent", "a maximum of 10 Points per day, regardless of the number of orders". Excludes "alcoholic beverages, delivery fees, gratuities (tips), gift card purchases, or taxes". 10-point signup bonus. "Customers do not earn Points for Orders Placed by Phone or In-Person."
- **Expiration:** points "not redeemed within one hundred eighty (180) consecutive calendar days from the date they are earned will be forfeited". **Redemptions:** "All point redemptions are final": https://www.jetspizza.com/rewards-terms-and-conditions/
- **Ladder (third-party summary, paraphrase):** Cinnamon Stix 30, ranch or cookies 40, 4 Corner Pizza 50, large 1-topping 70. At launch in 2020 the program earned on phone and text orders too: https://www.qsrmagazine.com/news/jets-pizza-rolls-out-rewards-program/

### MOD Pizza MOD Rewards
- **Earn:** "1 point for each One Dollar ($1.00) you spend". No points on "alcohol or gift cards, tax, gratuities, promotional discounts, purchases made by redeeming your Reward Points, or purchases made through third-party meal delivery services".
- **Expiration:** "REWARD POINTS EXPIRE ON A ROLLING BASIS ONE (1) YEAR FROM THE DATE THEY ARE EARNED."
- **Abuse:** accounts can be closed if "you have more than one (1) account": https://modpizza.com/rewards-and-promo-terms/
- **2023 ladder:** 50 points (cake, drink, crust upgrade, $1 donation), 100 (cheesy garlic bread, free delivery), 150 (any pizza or salad). The first reward dropped from 150 to 50 points (paraphrase): https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/mod-pizza-lowers-rewards-threshold-for-drinks-cheesy-garlic-bread/653071/
- **Effective rate (my calc):** 150 points for a pizza of about $12 is about 8%.

### Pizza chain takeaways
- The three biggest chains all moved to **more, cheaper rungs and a first reward within one or two orders** (Domino's 20 points, MOD 50, Marco's $50).
- Effective rates sit at **8 to 13%**, higher than coffee and fast casual (section 2), because a pizzeria ticket is larger and orders are less frequent.
- Nearly all exclude **tax, tips, gift cards, alcohol, and third-party marketplace orders**. Delivery fees split: Pizza Hut counts them, Marco's, Jet's and Papa Johns do not.

## 2. Restaurant leaders

### Starbucks Rewards (tiers reintroduced March 2026)
- **Tiers:** Green (under 500 Stars a year, 1 Star per $1), Gold (500+, 1.2 per $1), Reserve (2,500+, 1.7 per $1): https://thekrazycouponlady.com/tips/money/starbucks-rewards-program . The Points Party: Gold earns "1.2 Stars per dollar", Reserve "1.7 Stars per dollar base": https://thepointsparty.com/articles/starbucks-rewards-changes-2026-elite-tiers-devaluation . The changes took effect March 10, 2026 (paraphrase, search summary of Newsweek).
- **Ladder:** 25 Stars "Customize your drink with syrup or an extra espresso shot"; 60 Stars (new) "$2 off any menu item"; 100 Stars "A free iced/hot coffee or tea, bakery item, or packaged snack"; 200 Stars "A free handcrafted drink or hot breakfast item"; 300 Stars "Free sandwich, protein box, or at-home coffee"; 400 Stars merchandise up to $20 (Krazy Coupon Lady).
- **Expiration:** "Green Stars expire after 6 months, but any qualifying activity (a purchase counts) extends every Star in the account by another month." Gold and Reserve Stars "do not expire as long as they hold status" (The Points Party). Official terms: "Stars don't expire as long as you maintain your Gold or Reserve Level status.": https://www.starbucks.com/rewards/terms
- **Birthday:** "you must join Starbucks Rewards at least seven (7) days prior to your birthday, provide your birthday in your account information, and make at least one Star-earning transaction prior to your birthday each year." (starbucks.com terms). Gold gets a "7-day birthday reward window", Reserve a "30-day birthday window" (The Points Party).
- **Double Star Days:** Gold gets "at least four extra Double Star Days a year", Reserve "at least six more Double Star Days than Gold" (The Points Party).
- **Earning exclusions:** "Taxes, tips, donations, and fees, including without limitation, shipping and handling fees, gift wrapping fees, delivery fees, and bag fees." Voided purchases deduct Stars and can create negative balances (paraphrase, starbucks.com terms).
- **History of devaluation:** in February 2023 a handcrafted drink went from 150 to 200 Stars, 50 to 100, and 200 to 300 (paraphrase, search summary): https://onemileatatime.com/news/starbucks-rewards-devaluing/ . The Points Party calls the 2026 change a cut: old members were "effectively earning 2 Stars per dollar", while new Green members "top out at 1.5 Stars per dollar...a 25% cut."
- **Effective rate (my calc):** 200 Stars ($200) for a drink of about $6.50 is about 3%. Starbucks runs a low base rate and relies on frequency and bonus days.

### Chipotle Rewards
- **Earn:** "Every dollar spent on your real food faves gets you 10 points closer to your next reward."
- **Ladder:** side tortilla 85, chips 350, fountain drink 400, guac 500, double protein 700, 50% off an entree 815, entree 1,625, entree and chips 1,775.
- **Extras:** "Extras are how you level up your rewards. Take on challenges, earn extra points, and unlock exclusive badges along the way." Birthday: choose a free chips, guac, queso or drink. Expiration: "Points don't expire" with "One qualifying purchase a year": https://www.chipotle.com/rewards
- **Challenges in practice:** badges and 25 to 125 bonus points for spending a set amount or placing a set number of orders in a period (paraphrase): https://www.marketingdive.com/news/chipotle-gamifies-loyalty-program-with-extra-perks/605936/
- **Devaluation:** in October 2022 the entree went from 1,400 to 1,625 points. Chipotle: "To account for the additional cost of our real ingredients, we've marginally increased point values for menu items in the Chipotle Rewards Exchange." It launched at 1,250 in 2019: https://www.yahoo.com/news/chipotle-raising-prices-loyalty-rewards-170927018.html
- **Effective rate (my calc):** $162.50 for an entree of about $11.50 is about 7%. The 815-point "50% off" rung shows how a percent-off reward fills the gap below a full entree.

### Sweetgreen SG Rewards (April 2025)
- 10 points per dollar. It replaced Sweetpass, which had "consumer challenges and a subscription tier" ($100 a year), because consumers found it "too complicated". New members got 1,000 bonus points (a free Ripple Fries) on their first purchase: https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/sweetgreen-points-based-sg-rewards-ripple-fries/744283/ . Sweetgreen then added 20,000 new digital customers a week (paraphrase, search summary).

### Panera MyPanera (points from August 2026)
- Moved from surprise rewards based on visits to 10 points per $1, rolled out August 19, 2026. 250 points buys a bagel, side or baked treat, and 2,000 points buys an entree. MyPanera+ unlocks at $300 a year in spend and adds a free birthday You Pick Two and bonus delivery points. 500-point launch bonus on the first transaction. Chief Digital Officer Joshua Fine: "It's going to be even easier to earn rewards, faster to get them, and then you're going to have a great deal of choice in terms of how you redeem those rewards.": https://www.nrn.com/fast-casual/panera-bread-rolls-out-new-and-improved-loyalty-program
- **Lesson:** Panera and Sweetgreen both dropped opaque or complex models for plain points.

### Taco Bell Rewards
- Hot tier 10 points per $1. Fire tier at 2,000 points in a calendar year ($200), 11 per $1. "Every Taco Bell Rewards redemption costs the same 250 points." Points "expire six months after they're earned", and rewards are "only valid for 31 days after redeeming". The Points Guy estimates about 8% back for Hot and 16% for Fire (Aug 2026): https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/taco-bell-rewards/
- Removing Nachos BellGrande from the Fire menu in April 2023 drew complaints: "Nachos BellGrande is a huge loss RIP. It was nice while it lasted": https://www.eatthis.com/fast-food-rewards-programs-that-got-worse/

### McDonald's MyMcDonald's Rewards (unverified)
- 100 points per $1. Six redemption tiers in 2026 from 1,500 to about 7,000 points. Points can be redeemed for Expedia discounts since December 2025 (paraphrase, search summaries of third-party guides only; mcdonalds.com returned 403).

### Dutch Bros Rewards
- "3 points for each dollar you spend on qualifying purchases". "Free Medium Drink" 250 and "Free Drink of Your Choice" 325. "Points expire 180 days from the date you earned them". Excludes "Gift cards, Online purchases, Taxes, Tips, or Charity donations": https://www.dutchbros.com/support/articles/how-do-points-work/ . New members get a free medium drink valid 14 days (paraphrase, search summary).

### Chick-fil-A One
- 10 points per $1 base. Silver "Starts at 1,000 points" (11 per $1), Red "Starts at 4,000 points" (12), Signature "Starts at 10,000 points" (13). Rewards "start at only 200 points" (Icedream cone), with 12-count nuggets at 1,200. Silver and Red can "gift rewards to family and friends": https://www.chick-fil-a.com/one
- "Chick-fil-A One® reward points do not expire, but points toward member tier status will reset to zero on January 1st each calendar year.": https://www.chick-fil-a.com/customer-support/chick-fil-a-one-membership-program/benefits-and-tiers/do-chick-fil-a-one-reward-points-expire-at-the-end-of-the-year

## 3. Platforms independents use

### Toast Loyalty
- "Loyalty points are earned on the post-discount, pre-tax amount for all items." Gift cards earn nothing. Visit-based and amount-based programs are both supported, with automatic conversion between them. When several double-point promotions apply, "Toast automatically applies the one that gives them the most points." Birthday reward "becomes available on the guest's account 24 hours after they add their birthday". Enrollment needs "an email address or phone number". A "Loyalty Misuse Report" exists for suspected fraud: https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Toast-Loyalty-FAQ-1492794694913
- Points accrue fractionally, so a $10 spend on a "1 point per $15" program gives 0.66 points (paraphrase, search summary): https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/How-Guests-Earn-Points-with-Toast-Loyalty

### Square Loyalty
- **Program types:** visit (optional minimum spend, e.g. "Earn one point for each visit, with a $5 minimum purchase required"), spend (e.g. "Earn one point for every $2 dollars spent"), item, and category. Only spend programs can exclude items (paraphrase of search summary of Square developer docs): https://developer.squareup.com/docs/loyalty/overview . Up to 15 reward tiers: https://squareup.com/help/ca/en/article/6465-square-loyalty-get-started-guide
- **Phone enrollment and SMS:** "You enroll by entering your phone number and your earned rewards will be associated with this phone number". Members get "a welcome SMS message that includes a link to your status page" and sign in to the status page with a code sent by SMS ("Request Loyalty Code"). "You'll only be given the option to enroll after making a purchase eligible for a reward": https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6523-how-customers-track-their-loyalty-status
- **Expiration:** "You can choose to expire points after a set number of months." "Customers will receive an SMS notification 14 days before their points expire.": https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6462-square-loyalty-faqs
- **Refunds:** "Square also automatically adjusts loyalty points when an order is refunded, depending on the program type and refund type." (developer docs). Square staff in 2019: "We do not un-redeem the reward because we believe not all businesses would want to unredeem rewards." A seller replied that points and rewards "should be added back" symmetrically: https://community.squareup.com/t5/Customer-Engagement/Do-loyalty-points-get-taken-away-when-a-refund-is-initiated/td-p/132954
- **Fraud:** a daily email if "customer loyalty account has more than 5 loyalty transactions per day". The threshold is editable but "can't be lower than 2": https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8410-monitor-suspicious-activity-with-your-loyalty-program

### Owner.com
- Members join with "their phone, email and name. No usernames or passwords to remember." Owners "can adjust the point values for your menu items". Case studies on the page cite Metro Pizza at "+54% Sales growth", but loyalty's share of that is not separated: https://www.owner.com/loyalty-rewards

### Slice Rewards
- "Every $15+ order at participating shops on the Slice app gets you a Pizza Point." "8 Pizza Points = 1 free pizza!" (a large cheese). Points expire after six months without a qualifying order, and the credit is valid one year (paraphrase): https://slicelife.com/pages/slice-rewards . Older material lists middle rungs at 2, 4 and 6 points (topping, soda, garlic knots) (paraphrase): https://blog.slicelife.com/pizza-rewards-program/
- **Who pays:** "Slice Rewards is funded directly by your pizzeria." (edited March 2, 2026): https://slice.com/blog/what-is-slice-rewards/
- **Effective rate (my calc):** about $18 of pizza for $120 or more of spend is about 15% at the minimum, falling as orders grow. A Mink's program has to beat that for customers who also see the shop on Slice.

### Thanx
- Card-linked: the guest links a card once and purchases are tracked without a phone number at the counter. Supports tiers and non-discount perks such as secret menu access (paraphrase, search summaries of comparison guides): https://www.orderout.co/blog/best-loyalty-programs-for-restaurants/

### PAR Punchh (fraud reference)
- The most detailed public fraud playbook found. Countermeasures quoted from the page: "limiting registrations by device ID, requiring unique phone numbers, and holding rewards until guests prove they're genuinely engaged" for signup farming; "lock birthday fields after sign-up so guests can't continually change dates to chase more offers"; "add a delay between when a transaction happens and when points are issued, so if an order is canceled, the system automatically prevents points from being awarded"; "tier referral rewards based on volume and even cut off incentives entirely when after a suspicious volume of referrals occurs"; offers with "a unique ID that's also only valid for a short period, like 15 minutes": https://punchh.com/blog/2025/08/18/stop-loyalty-fraud-before-it-starts-how-par-engagement-protects-your-program-and-your-bottom-line/

### Paytronix
- Members spend "38% more per visit" and visit "20% more frequently" than non-members. "Once a member completes four visits, the return rate climbs to 95%", and "First-time guests return less than half the time": https://www.paytronix.com/blog/restaurant-loyalty-program-roi-guide . "Restaurant loyalty programs with online ordering see an 18% increase in order frequency": https://www.paytronix.com/resources/reports/restaurant-loyalty-insights-report . Vendor data, so likely biased toward programs that work.

### Spendgo
- 1 point per $1, a birthday item valid 30 days, referral tracking (paraphrase, search summaries). Olo acquired Spendgo in late 2025 (paraphrase): https://foodondemand.com/12222025/olo-acquires-spendgo-to-expand-platform-with-loyalty/

## 4. Cross-cutting findings

### Earn rate norms and effective reward rate
| Program | Earn | Headline reward | Effective rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domino's | 10 pts/order ≥$5 | medium 2-top at 60 | ~9% at $25 orders (my calc) |
| Pizza Hut | 2 pts/$ | medium 2-top at 200 | 9 to 20%+ (Points Insider) |
| Papa Johns | 1 pt/$ | $10 at 75 | 13.3% |
| Marco's | 1 pt/$ | $5 at 50 | 10% |
| MOD | 1 pt/$ | pizza at 150 | ~8% (my calc) |
| Slice | 1 pt/$15+ order | large cheese at 8 | ~15% max (my calc) |
| Taco Bell | 10 pts/$ | item at 250 | ~8% Hot, ~16% Fire (TPG) |
| Chipotle | 10 pts/$ | entree at 1,625 | ~7% (my calc) |
| Starbucks Green | 1 Star/$ | handcrafted at 200 | ~3% (my calc) |

- A practitioner benchmark puts sustainable reward cost at about 4 to 8% of member sales (paraphrase, search summary): https://www.katalystos.com/blog/how-to-launch-a-restaurant-loyalty-program-that-pays-for-itself
- 10 points per $1 is now the most common display convention (Chipotle, Taco Bell, Sweetgreen, Panera, Chick-fil-A). Larger numbers feel more generous and allow fine-grained bonuses.

### Redemption ladder design
- Every 2023 to 2026 relaunch added **a cheap first rung reachable in one or two orders** (Domino's 20, MOD 50, Chipotle 85, Starbucks 25 and 60, Panera 250, Chick-fil-A 200).
- **Item rewards beat dollar-off for perceived value** at chains with pricing power, but Marco's, Papa Johns and Starbucks (new 60-Star "$2 off") show dollar-off rewards are easier to explain and do not break when menu prices change.
- Fixed-cost catalogs (Taco Bell, 250 for anything) are simple but invite cherry-picking of the most expensive item, and removing that item causes backlash (Taco Bell 2023).

### Tiers and status
- Tiers are used by the largest brands (Starbucks, Chick-fil-A, Taco Bell, Panera+) with annual resets. Sweetgreen dropped tiers as "too complicated". For a small program, tiers add complexity for few guests. Taco Bell's Fire tier at $200 a year is the simplest model: one threshold, one bonus.

### Birthday rewards
- Paytronix case: a free sandwich valid 30 days, emailed the Sunday before the birthday week, had a "47% Eat-Thru℠ rate (redemption rate)" and boosted visits "by 26% during their birthday month versus the prior month": https://www.paytronix.com/resources/case-studies/birthday
- Average birthday redemption is about 12%, clustered in 1 to 8% for small add-ons and 30 to 40% for a real free item or points (paraphrase, search summary of QSR Magazine): https://www.qsrmagazine.com/growth/fast-casual/for-restaurants-and-loyalty-its-now-a-race-to-the-top/
- Guardrails: Starbucks requires joining 7 days ahead and one paid transaction; Toast waits 24 hours after a birthday is added; Punchh locks the birthday field.

### Signup bonuses
- Sweetgreen 1,000 points (a side) on first purchase, Panera 500, Jet's 10 points (one order's worth), Dutch Bros a free drink valid 14 days. The norm is **a bonus paid on the first purchase, not on account creation**, which kills fake-account farming.

### Referral programs
- No major chain in this set runs a public refer-a-friend loyalty bonus. General guidance is give-$10-get-$10, a minimum first order of $25 to $50, and checks that the friend is new (paraphrase, search summary): https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/food-referral-program . Punchh recommends tiering and cutting off referral rewards at suspicious volume (above).

### Challenges, streaks and bonus days
- Chipotle Extras and Little Caesars Challenges both pay out for set purchase or visit counts in a window. Paytronix cites "a 54% lift in visits with a strategic bonus-points campaign" (landing page above). Starbucks uses Double Star Days as a tier perk. Toast picks the single best multiplier when several apply, so stacking is not an issue there.

### Expiration and inactivity
- Patterns found: rolling from earn date (MOD 1 year, Dutch Bros 180 days, Taco Bell 6 months, Jet's 180 days), inactivity-based (Domino's 180 days, Pizza Hut 6 months, Papa Johns 12 months, Chipotle 1 year), never (Marco's points, Chick-fil-A). Earned rewards and certificates expire faster (Marco's 60 days, Taco Bell 31 days).
- Inactivity-based expiration is the most guest-friendly version that still caps liability. Square sends an SMS 14 days before expiration.

### Refunds and cancellations
- Square removes points proportionally on refund but does not restore a redeemed reward automatically. Starbucks deducts Stars on voids and allows negative balances. Jet's: "All point redemptions are final". Punchh recommends delaying point issuance until the order cannot be canceled.

### Earning base
- Toast: post-discount, pre-tax. Marco's: pre-tax, excluding tip and delivery. Starbucks, Jet's, MOD and Dutch Bros exclude tax, tips and fees. Pizza Hut alone includes delivery fees. MOD also excludes "promotional discounts" and "purchases made by redeeming your Reward Points".

### Fraud and identity
- **Signup farming:** unique phone numbers, device limits, first-purchase gating (Punchh).
- **Phone typing at checkout / employee fraud:** "An employee might add their own rewards account to a real customer's order and later apply that reward to a cash order to pocket the discount as cash.": https://foodtecsolutions.com/blog/2023/03/23/employee-fraud-the-3-most-common-occurrences-and-what-you-can-do-about-it/ . A variant registers the store phone number as an account (paraphrase, search summary of FoodTec 2020).
- **Velocity:** Square flags more than 5 loyalty transactions per day. Jet's caps earning at one order per day.
- **Account sharing:** Jet's, MOD and Little Caesars limit one account per person. Chick-fil-A instead allows gifting rewards at Silver and above.
- **SMS OTP risk:** OTP endpoints attract SMS pumping (toll fraud). Twilio recommends rate limits per number range, disabling countries "that you do not plan to send messages to", and bot detection or CAPTCHA: https://www.twilio.com/docs/verify/preventing-toll-fraud

### Breakage
- Typical breakage across loyalty programs is 5 to 30%, with retail about 15 to 33% (paraphrase, search summary): https://www.voucherify.io/glossary/loyalty-breakage . Under ASC 606, unredeemed points are a liability and breakage is recognized as redemptions occur (paraphrase). For a single store, this mostly means: track outstanding points value in the database, and do not design the program to depend on breakage.

### Customer complaints
- **Devaluations** draw the loudest anger. Starbucks: "I literally stopped going to Starbucks because they've continued to dilute the reward program while raising prices considerably". Chipotle: "they've now devalued the rewards program." Dunkin' 2022: "spits in the face of your loyal customers" (all from https://www.eatthis.com/fast-food-rewards-programs-that-got-worse/ ).
- **Complexity and distance:** Starbucks 2026 backlash: "Customers just want to earn a free drink every once in a while. It's not that complicated." and "Remember when it was 12 transactions and you got a free drink.": https://www.newsweek.com/starbucks-revamps-rewards-program-2026-11659352
- **Expiring points:** no fetched source quoted this directly; it is implied by the industry shift to inactivity-based expiration and pre-expiry reminders.

### What independent pizzerias say works

## 5. Recommended feature set for Mink's Pizza

Assumptions: average online ticket about $30 to $35, a large one-topping about $17, and garlic knots or a dessert about $6 to $7. Adjust the item rungs to the real menu, keeping the effective rate near 10%. Ranked by expected impact for one location.

**1. Points per dollar, earned on the right base (Must).**
- **10 points per $1** on the post-discount food and drink subtotal. Exclude tax, tip, delivery fee, gift cards, alcohol, and the value of any redeemed reward. Fractional points accrue (Toast model).
- Why: matches the 10x convention customers already know. Spend-based beats per-order because a pizzeria ticket varies a lot (single slice versus party order), and it avoids Domino's $5-order arbitrage. The base matches Marco's, Toast, MOD and Starbucks.

**2. A short ladder with a first reward inside one order (Must).**
- **300 points:** free 2-liter soda or dipping sauce pack (about $3, ~10%).
- **700 points:** free garlic knots or dessert (about $7, ~10%).
- **1,500 points:** free large one-topping pizza (about $17, ~11%), or **$15 off** as an alternative.
- Why: the first reward arrives after one typical order, as at Domino's and MOD. A free large pizza after about five orders beats Slice (8) and Domino's (6 for a medium). Effective cost of 10 to 11% sits inside the pizza-chain 8 to 13% band, and above the 4 to 8% fast-casual band because pizza frequency is lower. Keep the dollar-off option so menu price changes never force a devaluation.

**3. Phone-first identity with SMS OTP and email magic link (Must).**
- Enroll with phone number and name, verified by a 6-digit SMS code. Offer an email magic link as the fallback. One account per verified phone.
- Throttle OTP: at most 3 sends per number per hour and 10 per IP per hour, US numbers only, CAPTCHA after 2 failures.
- Why: phone is how Square, Owner.com and Toast enroll guests, and SMS verification stops the "typing someone else's number" problem. The throttles follow Twilio's toll-fraud guidance.

**4. Points pending until fulfillment, with clean refund rules (Must).**
- Points post when the order is marked completed, not at payment. A refund reverses points proportionally. A reward used on an order canceled before preparation is restored. Balances may go negative after a refund (Starbucks model), which blocks earn-redeem-refund loops. Log every manual adjustment with the staff user and reason.
- Why: Punchh's delay-issuance rule, Square's proportional reversal, and the employee-fraud pattern from FoodTec.

**5. Signup bonus paid on first completed order (Must).**
- **200 bonus points** on the first completed order of $15 or more. A $15 first order then reaches the 300 rung (150 + 200 = 350), so every new member gets a reward on their second order.
- Why: Sweetgreen, Panera and Jet's all pay on first purchase, which removes the value of fake accounts.

**6. Birthday reward (Should).**
- **Free garlic knots or dessert (the 700-point item, about $7), valid 14 days** from the birthday week. Requires the birthday to be set at least 30 days earlier and one completed order in the past 12 months. The birthday field locks after it is saved; changes go through support.
- Why: a real free item gets 30 to 47% redemption, against 1 to 8% for token add-ons. The guardrails copy Starbucks, Toast and Punchh. A free pizza is generous but costly at a single store; the side drives an order that usually includes a pizza.

**7. Inactivity-based expiration with warnings (Should).**
- Points expire after **12 months with no completed order**. Any order resets the clock. Send email and SMS (with consent) warnings at 30 days and 7 days.
- Why: Domino's, Pizza Hut and Papa Johns use inactivity rules. 12 months is generous enough for an occasional pizza buyer and still caps liability. Square sends a 14-day warning; two warnings recover more lapsed guests.

**8. Bonus days and one visit goal (Should).**
- **Double points on the slowest day** (for example Tuesday), never stacking with other multipliers (Toast rule). One standing challenge: **3 orders in 30 days earns 300 bonus points**.
- Why: Paytronix reports a 54% visit lift from a bonus-points campaign, and members who reach their fourth visit return 95% of the time. The 3-order goal pushes new members toward that fourth visit.

**9. Referral (Could, after launch).**
- The friend gets **$5 off a first order of $25 or more**. The referrer gets **500 points** once that order is completed and 7 days pass with no refund. The friend's phone must be new to the system. Cap at **10 rewarded referrals a year**, with manual review above 3 in a month.
- Why: no major chain in the set runs one, so evidence of lift is weak, but the cost is low at one location. The caps, holds and new-phone check follow Punchh and the general referral guidance.

**10. Abuse monitoring (Should).**
- Cap earning at **3 completed orders per account per day**. Alert the owner when an account earns on more than 3 orders in a day, more than 5 in a week, or has a staff member's phone number. Flag any account whose phone matches the store or staff numbers. Show a weekly report of manual point adjustments and rewards used on cash or phone orders.
- Why: Square's 5-a-day alert and Jet's one-a-day cap bracket the right range for a pizzeria, where two orders a day (lunch and dinner) is real.

**11. Tiers (Defer).**
- Do not launch with tiers. Revisit after 6 to 12 months of data. If added, use one tier: **"Regular" at $400 spent in a calendar year (about 12 orders), earning 12 points per $1**, plus an extended birthday window, reset each January 1 (Chick-fil-A model).
- Why: Sweetgreen dropped tiers as too complicated, and the Starbucks 2026 tiers caused backlash. At one store, too few guests would reach a tier for it to change behavior at launch.

**Rules to publish on day one.** Earning base and exclusions, expiration rule, one account per person, how refunds work, and a promise to give 60 days' notice before any change to reward costs. Starbucks, Chipotle, Taco Bell and Dunkin' show that devaluations do the most damage to trust, so launch at rates the shop can hold.
